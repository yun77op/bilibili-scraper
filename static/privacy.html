Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026 · 视频转文章 (bilibili-scraper)
https://bilibili-scraper.shuilong.uk

This service turns Bilibili and YouTube videos into readable articles. This policy explains what we store and how Notion access works.

1. Account data

When you register, we store your username and a hashed password (or a Google account identifier and email if you sign in with Google). We also store per-account settings such as YouTube cookies you paste, Notion parent page ID, and upload preferences.

2. Content you generate

Video URLs, transcripts, generated articles, job logs, and related files stay under your account. Other users cannot see them. Articles are intended for personal study; you remain responsible for respecting the original video’s copyright.

3. Notion connection

If you connect Notion, we use OAuth. You choose which pages to grant. We store your Notion access token on the server so we can create child pages under the parent page you configured. We do not request access to your entire Notion workspace. You can disconnect anytime in Settings; we then delete the stored token.

4. Cookies and session

We use a login session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

5. Third parties

Processing may call Bilibili or YouTube to fetch video metadata/subtitles/audio, DeepSeek to rewrite articles, Groq or a local Whisper model to transcribe audio, and the Notion API to create pages you requested. We are not affiliated with Bilibili, YouTube, Google, DeepSeek, or Notion.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email listed on the Notion connection page, or the site operator at https://bilibili-scraper.shuilong.uk

隐私政策（中文摘要）

我们只保存你的账号、你提交的视频任务、生成的文章，以及你主动授权的 Notion token。Notion 授权时由你勾选页面，不会申请整个工作区。可在设置页取消授权并删除 token。内容仅供个人学习，请尊重原视频版权。
